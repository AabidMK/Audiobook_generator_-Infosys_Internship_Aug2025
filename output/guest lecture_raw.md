                                          REPORT – A GUEST LECTURE  
 
Title of the Program  : Interactive MLOps for Engineers  
Date  : 13.08.2025 
Resource Person  : Mr. C. B. Veer kumar  
   Engineer ing Analyst (Tech lead ) 
   WIPRO Technologies  
  Coimbatore  
No of Beneficiaries  : 57 
Mr. C. B. Veerakumar, Engineering Analyst (Tech Lead – Software Development & 
Configuration) at Wipro Technologies, Coimbatore, delivered an engaging guest lecture on “MLOps 
for Engineers” for the Department of AI & DS students. The session was presided over by Dr. V. 
Karpagam, Prof & Head / AI&DS, who welcomed the gathering and emphasize d the value of learning 
industry -relevant skills.  
Mr. Veerakumar explained the fundamentals of MLOps, its role in integrating machine 
learning models into production, and its importance in improving collaboration between data scientists 
and operations teams. He discussed best practices, challenges, and benefits of adopting MLOps in real -
world applications.  
The lecture also highlighted the use of GitHub for version control, collaboration, and 
continuous integration, with practical insights into its role in MLOps workflows . The session was 
highly informative, offering valuable perspectives on current industry practices and equipping students 
with knowledge essential for their professional growth .
